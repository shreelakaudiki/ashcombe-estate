PRIVATE PAPERS · AE-HN-03

Household Notes

Domestic memoranda and household observations

Collection Household Notes
Period 1901–1925
Location Ashcombe Manor

Collection Description

The Household Notes consist primarily of short memoranda concerning the daily administration and maintenance of Ashcombe Manor.

Domestic Arrangements

Surviving notes concern meal arrangements, deliveries, household supplies and preparations for visiting members of the family.

Maintenance

Several memoranda refer to repairs carried out throughout the house, including work to fireplaces, windows, furniture and sections of the western wing.

Household Staff

The papers contain occasional references to the housekeeper, cook, maids, footman and gardener in connection with routine household duties.

Miscellaneous Notes

A small number of undated memoranda concern flowers for the drawing room, arrangements for evening guests and the preparation of rooms before family gatherings.

Archive Note

These notes were retained with the household papers and appear to have been maintained for practical domestic use. Their original order has not been preserved.
