Household Register

Domestic and household records

Year 1889
Location Ashcombe Manor
Register Household Papers

Record Description

A household register recording domestic arrangements and selected household personnel at Ashcombe Manor during the late nineteenth century.

Household Staff

The surviving register records the housekeeper, cook, two maids, a footman and a gardener. Additional seasonal staff were recorded separately.

Domestic Arrangements

The principal household rooms included the drawing room, library, dining room, morning room and several guest bedrooms. Maintenance records also refer to the kitchens, servants' quarters and garden buildings.

Household Inventory Note

Furniture and domestic objects were reviewed during the annual household inspection. Detailed object descriptions were not consistently entered into the register.
