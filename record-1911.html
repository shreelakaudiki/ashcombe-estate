HOUSEHOLD INVENTORY · AE-1911-07

Household Inventory

Domestic furnishings and household effects

Year 1911
Location Ashcombe Manor
Register Household Inventory

Record Description

A household inventory recording selected furniture, domestic furnishings and household effects held at Ashcombe Manor during 1911.

Principal Rooms

The inventory includes entries for the drawing room, dining room, library, morning room, study and several guest bedrooms.

Household Effects

Recorded objects include upholstered seating, writing desks, clocks, framed prints, silver tableware, books and assorted domestic furnishings.

Inventory Note

Several household objects were recorded only by room and category. Individual descriptions were not consistently maintained in the surviving inventory.
