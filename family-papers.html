Family Papers

Selected correspondence and domestic papers of the Ashcombe family

Collection Family Papers
Period 1880–1926
Location Ashcombe Manor

Collection Description

The Family Papers contain correspondence and miscellaneous household documents retained by successive members of the Ashcombe family.

Correspondence

The surviving papers include letters concerning family visits, invitations, household arrangements and correspondence with relatives living outside Surrey.

Estate Matters

Several documents concern repairs to the manor, arrangements for visiting guests and routine communications with the estate steward.

Household Papers

Additional papers include household lists, invitations, receipts and miscellaneous notes retained with the family's private correspondence.
