PRIVATE CORRESPONDENCE · AE-1908-12

Private Correspondence

Family and estate communications

Year 1908
Location Ashcombe Manor
Register Private Papers

Record Description

A small collection of correspondence concerning household arrangements, estate maintenance and visits by members of the Ashcombe family.

Correspondence Summary

The surviving letters discuss repairs to the western wing of the manor, arrangements for visiting relatives and the preparation of rooms for a summer gathering.

Estate Matters

Several letters refer to the maintenance of the billiards room, replacement of household linens and the seasonal inspection of the estate grounds. No detailed inventory of objects is included in this correspondence.

Correspondents

Correspondence was exchanged between members of the Ashcombe household, the estate steward and several local tradesmen.
