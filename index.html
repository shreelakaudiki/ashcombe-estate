ASHCOMBE MANOR

The Ashcombe Estate Archive contains selected inventories, household records and private papers relating to Ashcombe Manor and the Ashcombe family.

The surviving records cover the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, with particular attention given to the final years of the estate's private ownership.
