ESTATE INVENTORY · AE-1927-14

Estate Inventory

Final inventory before transfer of Ashcombe Manor

Year 1927
Location Ashcombe Manor
Register Estate Inventory

Record Description

Final household inventory prepared before the transfer of Ashcombe Manor from the Ashcombe family following financial difficulties.

Principal Rooms

The surviving inventory records furnishings and household effects from the drawing room, library, dining room, study, morning room and billiards room.

Billiards Room

The billiards room contained a billiards table, two cue stands, a card table, upholstered chairs and a small collection of playing cards.

The inventory records the playing set as incomplete. Six cards were originally kept with the card table, although only five were present when the inventory was prepared.

Private Papers

A small group of papers belonging to Eleanor Ashcombe was recorded separately from the household effects.

One item was removed from the inventory before the transfer of the estate. No description of the item was entered in the public inventory.

Inventory Note

The billiards room was traditionally used for evening gatherings and card games. The surviving household records indicate that the room remained in regular use during the final years of the Ashcombe family's residence.
