Record Description

Early estate documentation concerning the establishment of the Ashcombe family's country residence and surrounding grounds.

Estate Note

The Ashcombe family acquired the property in the early nineteenth century. Surviving records from this period primarily concern land boundaries, agricultural use and the maintenance of the principal residence.

Recorded Holdings

The original foundation register refers to household furniture, agricultural equipment, silverware and several private family effects. No detailed object inventory survives from this period.
